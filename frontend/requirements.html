Bingham University Admission Criteria

Comprehensive guide to Institutional and Departmental requirements for the 2025/2026 Academic Session.

🏛️ Institutional Minimum Requirements

  • O'Level Credits: Candidates must possess a minimum of five (5) O'Level credit passes (WAEC, NECO, NABTEB, or IGCSE) obtained at not more than two (2) sittings.
  • Mandatory Subjects: Mathematics and English Language are compulsory for ALL courses.
  • JAMB UTME: Candidates must have written the 2025/2026 JAMB UTME. The general university cut-off mark is 150 (Note: Medicine & Surgery, Nursing, and Law require a higher cut-off of 190+).
  • Age Requirement: Candidates must be at least 16 years of age by October 31st of the admission year.
