How to Apply

  • Create an account using your JAMB Registration Number and a valid email address.
  • Log in to your portal to access the application form.
  • Fill in your academic records and UTME score accurately.
  • Upload a clear image of your O'Level result for AI-powered verification.
  • Submit your application and await the screening result.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Minimum UTME score of 160 (course-specific cut-offs apply).
  • Minimum of 5 O'Level credits including Mathematics and English.
  • Valid JAMB Registration Number.
